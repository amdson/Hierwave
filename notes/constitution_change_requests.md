## 1. Coarse levels trained on probe-lift contexts (from sweep2b, ruled out-of-bounds by the orchestrator)
**What:** `ckernel` reached own-draw line_match 0.62 (first variant above 0.50), v2 probe rho 0.82-0.86 at levels 1-3 and starvation <= 10 % by applying the kernels to a 50/50 mix of the sampler's own draws and probe lifts, and fitting ALL levels (including 3-4) by ML on the refined states. An own-draws-only run stays collapsed (dirt 0.90, line_match 0.50). So the gain at the coarse levels comes from imitating the probe lifts' coarse channels, refined by the kernels.
**Why it is outside the frozen rules:** the amendment admits ML on states produced by p*-invariant kernels applied to the sampler's OWN draws; ideas #14 admits training the fine levels CONDITIONED on arbitrary parent states (probe lifts included), not training the coarse levels to reproduce the probes' coarse states. Probe lifts are designed, target-like states supplied by the problem, so this is training on a hand-made sample of the target's coarse marginal (constraint 1 in spirit).
**Ruling for the session:** the probe-context arm is kept as a labelled diagnostic upper bound ("what the coarse levels do when given target-like coarse states"); the main line must get own draws out of the dirt state with kernels applied to own draws (coarse flips, annealed blocks). Both arms are reported.
**If the user wants to admit it:** a sentence in the amendment allowing kernel-refined probe lifts as training contexts for all levels, on problems that declare probes; the bench's probe check then no longer tests generalisation to unseen target-like states and should use held-out probes (different jitter / seeds) from those used in training.

## 2. Maximum likelihood plus a penalty-only violation term (from wave2-bench and dimers-kernel part 2)
**What:** on both ground and dimers the kernel route now shows the same trade-off. ML alone on kernel-refined states gives diversity (ground: own-draw line_match 0.62-0.70, starvation <= 10 %; dimers: three orientations .36/.32/.32) but leaks mass onto violating states (ground 3.2e-3, dimers 5.6e-2 per cell), because forward KL never penalises mass where the data has none. Mixing cfast's full reverse-KL update restores validity (ground 5e-5..4.3e-4; dimers 3.4e-4) but brings the mode-seeking pressure back with it (ground starvation 24/42 % at L3/L4; dimers collapses to two orientations, diagonal 0.000-0.011).
**Proposal:** train with J' = −Σ_i w_i log q(x_i) + Σ_l λ_l E_q[H_l], i.e. ML plus ONLY the penalty part of the reverse-KL objective (score-function gradient of the expected violation count under q, with cfast's adaptive λ_l ≤ 20, critic as control variate), without the designed-energy and entropy terms that carry the collapse pressure. This is the natural constrained-ML objective: match the kernel states, subject to the violation budget.
**Why it is a change:** the amendment admits the ML objective and admits mixing the full reverse-KL update; a partial reverse-KL term is a different, new objective (constraint 4 as amended; constraint 3's "no dropped cost terms" was written for the reverse-KL gradient). Not built during the frozen session.
**If adopted:** one sentence in the amendment: "the penalty term Σ_l λ_l E_q[H_l] may be added to the weighted-ML objective on its own, with the same λ control and the same unbiased estimator as in reverse-KL training." First test: ckernel on ground with the penalty term in place of the reverse-KL mix, two seeds, v2 bench; dimers the same.

## 3. Violation price on the repaired output at the coarsest level (from wave5-pop)
